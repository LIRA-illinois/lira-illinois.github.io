---
title: "Our paper on contraction-aware RL was accepted to T-RO!"
date: 2026-08-11
---

Our paper titled ["Contraction-Aware Reinforcement Learning for Nonlinear Control with Statistical Robustness"](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2506.15700) was accepted to [IEEE Transactions on Robotics (T-RO)](https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/xpl/RecentIssue.jsp?punumber=8860)! We propose a method that integrates contraction theory with reinforcement learning to provide long-term optimaility with stability guarantees. The work was led by MJ Cho.
